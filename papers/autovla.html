one-line summary한 줄 요약 Existing driving VLAs either emit waypoints as text (physically infeasible, slow, imprecise) or bolt a separate planner/decoder onto the VLM (breaks end-to-end training). AutoVLA instead clusters 2,048 real half-second vehicle manoeuvres into an action codebook and adds them to the LLM vocabulary, so planning becomes plain next-token prediction. It is then trained to run in two modes — direct trajectory (fast) or chain-of-thought first (slow) — and a GRPO stage with a CoT-length penalty teaches it which mode each scene deserves. Result: +10.6% PDMS and −66.8% runtime over its own SFT baseline, and state of the art on the CARLA Bench2Drive closed-loop benchmark (78.84 driving score / 57.73% success). 기존 VLA는 좌표를 텍스트로 쓰거나(느리고 부정확하며 물리적으로 불가능한 궤적이 나온다) 별도 planner를 붙인다(end-to-end 학습이 끊긴다). AutoVLA는 실제 0.5초 동작 2,048개로 만든 액션 코드북을 vocab에 추가해 계획을 next-token prediction으로 바꾸고, fast·slow 두 모드를 익힌 뒤 CoT 길이 페널티를 넣은 GRPO로 모드 선택을 배운다. 결과는 SFT 대비 PDMS +10.6%, 런타임 −66.8%, 그리고 CARLA Bench2Drive closed-loop 1위(78.84점, 성공률 57.73%)다.

Open-loop: replay a recording, compare the model's answer to the human's. The car never actually moves. Closed-loop: the model's output drives, the world changes, mistakes compound. Much harder, much more meaningful.open-loop는 녹화를 재생하며 모델 답을 사람 답과 비교한다. closed-loop는 모델 출력대로 실제 주행해 실수가 누적되므로 훨씬 어렵다.

1 Problem definition 문제 정의

1.1 — Why driving people started wanting a language model at all 1.1 자율주행이 언어 모델을 원하게 된 이유

Classical autonomous driving is a pipeline: perception → prediction → planning. Clean to build, but it has two structural problems the paper opens with. Errors accumulate down the chain (perception is 5% wrong, prediction is wrong on top of that, planning on top of that), and each module optimises its own loss, so nothing optimises driving quality. End-to-end models (camera in, trajectory out) fix both — but they learn purely by imitating expert trajectories, so they carry no world knowledge:

고전적 자율주행은 인지 → 예측 → 계획 파이프라인이라 오차가 누적되고(인지가 5% 틀리면 그 위에서 또 틀린다), 아무도 주행 품질 자체를 최적화하지 않는다. end-to-end 모델은 이를 해결하지만 전문가 궤적을 모방할 뿐이라 세상 지식이 없다.

"However, conventional end-to-end methods [16–19] primarily focus on imitating expert trajectories, lacking essential world knowledge for understanding and reasoning about surrounding environments, particularly in long-tail or challenging scenarios."

— §1, ¶2 · 원문 보기 (§1 Introduction)

"Long-tail" here means the scene the paper keeps returning to: a construction worker holding a SLOW paddle while a forklift blocks the right lane. An imitation-trained model has probably never seen it. A pretrained VLM already knows what a construction worker, a paddle and a forklift are — that is the entire bet.

"long-tail"의 대표 예는 공사장 인부가 SLOW 표지판을 들고 지게차가 우측 차선을 막은 장면이다. 모방 모델은 본 적 없겠지만 사전학습 VLM은 인부·표지판·지게차를 이미 안다. 이 논문은 여기에 건다.

The abstract names the failure modes bluntly, and the rest of the paper is organised around fixing exactly these:

1.3 — Take (c) seriously for a moment: why can't an LLM just write coordinates? 1.3 LLM이 좌표를 직접 쓰면 왜 안 되나

This is the most instructive failure, and the one closest to home. Ask a VLM for "the next 5 seconds as waypoints" and it emits -0.03, 1.47, … as sequences of digit tokens. Three things go wrong at once: precision is poor (LLMs are bad at numbers), it burns a lot of tokens (slow), and — worst — nothing constrains the output to motions a car can physically perform. The paper's word for the resulting collapse is mode collapse:

우리 일과 가장 가까운 실패다. VLM이 -0.03, 1.47 같은 waypoint를 숫자 토큰으로 쓰면 정밀도가 낮고 느리다. 무엇보다 차가 실제로 할 수 있는 움직임이라는 보장이 없다. 논문은 이를 mode collapse라고 부른다.

"Some models generate textual actions or waypoints directly using VLMs [35–37], but these outputs can be physically infeasible and suffer from mode collapse."

— §1, ¶3 · 원문 보기 (§1)

The obvious repair is to let the VLM emit something abstract (a meta-action, a latent token) and hand it to a real planner or decoder — paradigms (a) and (b). But then you have either two models or a severed gradient. And when people did put a proper generative planner inside the VLM, they paid for it in complexity:

흔한 수선책은 VLM이 meta-action 같은 추상 출력을 내고 별도 planner가 이어받는 것이다(패러다임 (a), (b)). 그러면 모델이 둘이 되거나 gradient가 끊긴다. VLM 안에 생성형 planner를 넣으면 복잡도가 커진다.

"However, simple trajectory decoders employed in these methods (e.g., MLP [41, 84] or GRU [45]) may produce impractical trajectories and suffer from modal collapse. To address this issue, ORION [42] incorporates generative planners into VLM architectures, enhancing trajectory feasibility but increasing model complexity and computational demands."

— §2, "VLA and VLM for Autonomous Driving" · 원문 보기 (§2 Related Work)

And on the reasoning side, DriveVLM had already tried the fast/slow idea — but with two separate systems, which is what AutoVLA sets out to collapse into one:

추론 쪽에서는 DriveVLM이 이미 fast/slow를 시도했지만 시스템 두 개로 나눴다. AutoVLA는 이를 하나로 합치려 한다.

"Although DriveVLM [46] introduces a dual-process paradigm, it relies on separate modules (i.e., a VLM for slow reasoning and a conventional end-to-end model for fast responses), which results in a complicated architecture, increased training overhead, and limited scalability [47]."

— §1, ¶3 · 원문 보기 (§1)

So the shopping list for §2 is fixed: an action representation that is feasible by construction, kept inside the language model, with both thinking speeds in one set of weights.

그래서 §2의 과제가 정해진다. 구조적으로 실현 가능한 행동 표현을 언어 모델 안에 두고, 두 생각 속도를 하나의 가중치에 담는 것이다.

2 Method 해결 방법

2.1 — The core move: build a dictionary of manoeuvres, then treat driving as spelling 2.1 핵심 아이디어: 동작 사전을 만들고 주행을 받아쓰기로 바꾼다

This is the one idea to take away. Instead of writing coordinates, the authors discretise trajectories: chop motion into 0.5-second pieces, collect the pieces real cars actually produce, keep 2,048 distinctive ones, and register each as a token in the vocabulary.

이 논문의 핵심이다. 궤적을 0.5초 조각으로 이산화하고, 실제 차량 조각 중 서로 구별되는 2,048개를 골라 vocab 토큰으로 등록한다.

"To enable trajectory planning within the language model, we discretize continuous vehicle trajectories … into a sequence of physical action tokens … where each token is represented by short-term spatial position and heading movement (∆x, ∆y, ∆θ). This transforms the planning task into a next-token prediction problem, which can be conducted within the language model."

— §3.1, "Action Tokenization" · 원문 보기 (§3.1)

The selection rule is called K-disk clustering, and it is refreshingly simple — a greedy "keep it only if it's different enough from everything I already kept" pass over real driving data:

선별 규칙은 K-disk clustering이다. 실제 주행 데이터를 훑으며 "이미 담은 것과 충분히 다를 때만 담는" 단순한 greedy 방식이다.

"Then, we apply K-Disk clustering on these sampled segments, which iteratively selects a diverse set of representative segments {m1, . . . , mK}, such that no two segments are within a distance threshold δ = 0.05 m, measured using average contour distance. … The resulting action codebook is A = {a1, . . . , aK}, with K = 2048, where each token encodes a distinct and physically feasible short-term vehicle behavior."

— §A.1 (Supplementary), ¶2 · 원문 보기 (§A.1 Action Codebook)

Note where the segments come from: the Waymo Open Motion Dataset, i.e. logs of real vehicles. And note the distance measure — not centre-point distance but average contour distance, comparing the car's whole bounding-box outline. Two manoeuvres count as "the same" only if the vehicle's footprint ends up in nearly the same place and orientation.

조각은 실제 차량 로그인 Waymo Open Motion Dataset에서 왔다. 거리는 bounding box 외곽 전체를 비교하는 평균 외곽선 거리로 잰다.

why this kills the "infeasible output" problem outright"물리적으로 불가능한 출력"이 원천 봉쇄되는 이유 Every entry in the dictionary was performed by a real car. So whatever token the model emits — even a wrong or badly-chosen one — is still a motion a car can execute. Feasibility is no longer something the model has to learn; it is a property of the output alphabet. Paradigm (c) could never guarantee this no matter how well it was trained. 사전의 모든 항목은 실제 차가 수행한 동작이라, 틀린 토큰을 골라도 실행 가능한 움직임이다. 실현 가능성은 학습할 대상이 아니라 출력 알파벳의 성질이 된다. 패러다임 (c)는 이를 보장하지 못한다.

Fig. 3 · Action tokenization end to end. Based on paper §3.1 + §A.1–A.2; panel ② is a schematic of the codebook's reachable set (a car can only turn sharply when it is slow).그림 3 · 액션 토크나이제이션 전체 흐름. 논문 §3.1, §A.1–A.2 기반. 패널 ②는 코드북 도달 영역의 개념도다(차는 느릴 때만 급하게 돌 수 있다).

The round trip is spelled out in the supplementary, and it is worth reading twice because it is the part people usually get wrong when reimplementing:

왕복 과정은 supplementary에 나온다. 재구현할 때 자주 틀리는 부분이다.

"During training, continuous trajectories are discretized by mapping each 0.5-second segment to its nearest action token in the codebook A, resulting in a sequence of discrete tokens [a1, a2, . . . , aT ]. During inference, the language model autoregressively generates a sequence of action tokens, each representing a 0.5-second motion segment. These tokens are then converted back to their corresponding motion segments in the codebook and applied sequentially from the initial ego pose."

— §A.2 (Supplementary), "Action Tokenizer" · 원문 보기 (§A.2)

Two consequences fall out of the fixed 0.5 s granularity. The output length is constant — a 5-second plan is always exactly 10 tokens, so there is no "how long should the answer be" question for the action part. And the tokenizer imposes a hard ceiling on accuracy: however good the policy is, it can never be more precise than the codebook's own reconstruction error. That ceiling is what the next table measures.

0.5초 고정 단위라서 출력 길이가 상수다(5초 = 토큰 10개). 또 토크나이저가 정확도 상한을 정한다. 정책이 아무리 좋아도 코드북 복원 오차보다 정밀할 수 없고, 다음 표가 이 상한을 잰다.

One more practical detail: because simulated vehicles don't move like real ones, the authors built a second codebook for CARLA using the same procedure — same recipe, different physics, 2,048 tokens again. If you ever port this to a new vehicle platform, this is the step you have to redo.

시뮬레이션 차량은 다르게 움직이므로 CARLA용 두 번째 코드북(역시 2,048개)을 같은 절차로 만들었다. 새 차량 플랫폼에서는 이 단계를 다시 해야 한다.

Read the two ends of the table. At 256 the dictionary is too small — 13.5% of real manoeuvres have no adequate entry, so error jumps. At 4,096 accuracy barely improves but 8.5% of tokens are dead weight, never emitted, occupying embedding rows and softmax mass for nothing. 2,048 is the knee:

"As the codebook size increases beyond K = 2048, the improvements in tokenization accuracy and movement coverage become marginal, while codebook usage decreases as many tokens remain redundant and unused. Considering this trade-off, we select K = 2048 to balance reconstruction accuracy, movement coverage, and efficient codebook usage."

2.3 — Two thinking speeds, one set of weights 2.3 생각 속도는 둘, 가중치는 하나

With actions living in the vocabulary, "reason then act" and "just act" become two response formats rather than two architectures. Fast mode emits a fixed one-line stub in the think block and goes straight to actions:

행동이 vocab 안에 있으면 "생각 후 행동"과 "바로 행동"은 응답 형식의 차이일 뿐이다. fast 모드는 think 블록에 고정된 한 줄만 넣는다.

<think> This is a straightforward scenario, and a direct decision can be made. </think>
<answer><action_22><action_12><action_26><action_34><action_31><action_33><action_68><action_53><action_42><action_58></answer>

Slow mode fills the same think block with a four-part chain of thought — scene description, critical objects, intent reasoning, final decision — and only then emits the same ten action tokens:

slow 모드는 같은 think 블록을 네 부분 CoT(장면 묘사, 핵심 객체, 의도 추론, 최종 판단)로 채운 뒤 같은 액션 토큰 10개를 낸다.

<think> This is a complex scenario requiring additional reasoning. 1. Scene Description … 2. Critical Object Description … 3. Reasoning on Intent … 4. Best Driving Action: change lane to the left with an acceleration. </think>

"In fast thinking mode, AutoVLA directly predicts physical action tokens without generating long CoT reasoning, enabling rapid responses in straightforward scenarios. In contrast, slow thinking mode involves structured CoT reasoning, where the model first analyzes the environment, identifies critical elements, and reasons through potential outcomes before deciding on the final driving action."

— §3.1, "Unified Reasoning and Action" · 원문 보기 (§3.1)

Because both modes end in exactly ten action tokens, the only thing that differs is how many language tokens were generated first. Which means the mode choice is purely a decode-length decision — and therefore purely a latency decision. Hold that thought until §3.2, where it turns into a 10× runtime gap.

두 모드 모두 액션 토큰 10개로 끝나므로 유일한 차이는 앞의 언어 토큰 수다. 모드 선택은 곧 레이턴시 결정이고, §3.2에서 10배 격차로 나타난다.

2.4 — Stage 1 (SFT): where does chain-of-thought training data for driving even come from? 2.4 1단계 (SFT): 자율주행 CoT 데이터는 어디서 구하나

Nobody has 45,000 hand-written explanations of why a car should slow down. The authors are candid about why existing reasoning datasets weren't usable:

"차가 왜 감속해야 하는가"를 손으로 쓴 설명 45,000개를 가진 사람은 없다. 저자들은 기존 리즈닝 데이터셋을 쓸 수 없던 이유를 이렇게 적는다.

"Despite its importance, the development of a high-quality, large-scale driving reasoning dataset remains a key challenge due to three major limitations: 1) limited scenario diversity and repetitive examples, 2) inadequate representation of critical perceptual cues, such as traffic signs and vehicle indicator signals, 3) low-quality reasoning process, such as repeatedly stopping at a stop sign without justification."

— §3.2, ¶1 · 원문 보기 (§3.2 Reasoning Data)

So they generated it — Qwen2.5-VL-72B as the teacher, Qwen2.5-VL-3B as the student. Straight knowledge distillation, except what's distilled is explanations rather than logits. The prompt forces a four-part structure (scene → critical objects → intent → decision), and there is one trick that makes the whole pipeline work:

그래서 Qwen2.5-VL-72B(선생님)로 Qwen2.5-VL-3B(학생)용 설명을 생성했다. 프롬프트는 네 부분 구조(장면 → 핵심 객체 → 의도 → 판단)를 강제하고, 핵심 트릭이 하나 있다.

"To regulate the reasoning outcomes, our approach incorporates ground-truth driving actions as hints, guiding the model to produce causal explanations that explicitly link driving decisions to scene context. This structured prompting method significantly reduces nonsensical outputs and minimizes the need for manual correction."

— §3.2, ¶3 · 원문 보기 (§3.2)

Read that carefully — the teacher is told the right answer and asked to justify it. It is not predicting; it is rationalising a known outcome. That's what keeps the CoT from wandering off, and it is the single most reusable idea in the paper for anyone building reasoning data. Volume produced: ~45.6k CoT annotations for nuPlan and 7.2k for Waymo E2E, plus DriveLM VQA pairs reformatted into CoT form for nuScenes and CARLA.

선생님은 정답을 미리 받고 그것을 정당화하므로 CoT가 엉뚱한 데로 새지 않는다. 리즈닝 데이터 생성에 가장 재사용하기 좋은 아이디어다. nuPlan CoT 주석 약 45.6k개, Waymo E2E 7.2k개에 DriveLM VQA 쌍을 CoT로 바꿔 보탰다.

λ_cot = 40 is a big number, and it tells you how lopsided the data is: reasoning examples are rare relative to trajectory-only examples, so each one is made to count 40×. Worth remembering when you read the data-scaling result in §3.3 — reasoning is the scarce, expensive ingredient here, not the default one.

4.2 — Our perspective: four things here transfer straight to LLM serving 4.2 우리 관점: LLM 서빙으로 바로 옮겨오는 네 가지

editor's note — not claims of the paper편집자 주: 논문의 주장 아님 Everything in this subsection is our reading of what the paper implies for the work this site is about, not something the authors argue. The driving results are theirs; the extrapolation is ours. 이 소절은 이 논문이 우리 작업에 주는 시사점에 대한 해석이지 저자들의 논증이 아니다. 주행 결과는 저자들의 것이고 확장 해석은 우리 것이다.

① Put output length in the reward① 보상에 출력 길이를 넣기

The − λ_r · r_CoT term is decode-latency optimisation done with RL instead of with a max_tokens cap. A hard cap truncates good reasoning and cheap reasoning alike; a learned penalty lets the model spend tokens where they pay. Directly applicable to any reasoning model that over-thinks easy prompts — see RL methods for LLMs.− λ_r · r_CoT 항은 max_tokens 상한 대신 RL로 하는 디코드 레이턴시 최적화다. 하드 캡은 좋은 리즈닝까지 자르지만 학습된 페널티는 필요한 곳에만 토큰을 쓰게 한다(LLM용 RL 방법 참고).

② Tail latency, not average latency② 평균이 아니라 tail latency

Look at Table 2 again: slow mode ranges 7.6 – 13.7 s. Average 10.5 s is not the operational problem — the 13.7 s worst case against a 2 Hz control loop is. A CoT-length penalty is therefore a p99 control mechanism, which is exactly the framing we use for SLO-aware routing.Table 2의 slow 모드는 7.6 – 13.7초다. 문제는 평균 10.5초가 아니라 최악 13.7초다. CoT 길이 페널티는 p99 제어 장치이고, SLO 기반 라우팅과 같은 관점이다.

③ Model-internal routing vs gateway routing③ 모델 내부 라우팅 vs 게이트웨이 라우팅

We solve "easy request → cheap path, hard request → expensive path" at the gateway. This paper solves it inside one set of weights — no classifier, no second model, no routing hop. The trade-off is real: gateway routing is observable and tunable at runtime; model-internal routing is cheaper and can't be inspected or overridden. Worth holding next to Heimdall vs Dynamo.우리는 "쉬운 요청은 싼 경로, 어려운 요청은 비싼 경로"를 게이트웨이에서 풀고, 이 논문은 가중치 하나 안에서 푼다. 게이트웨이 라우팅은 관측·조정할 수 있고, 모델 내부 라우팅은 싸지만 들여다보거나 덮어쓸 수 없다. Heimdall vs Dynamo와 함께 볼 만하다.

④ Don't make an LLM write numbers④ LLM에게 숫자를 쓰게 하지 말 것

The most portable lesson, and the cheapest to apply: when a task needs precise numeric output, extend the vocabulary instead of emitting digits. Table 5 got +9.23 PDMS and −48% runtime from that one change. Any structured-output task where you currently parse floats out of generated text is a candidate.가장 싸게 적용할 수 있는 교훈이다. 정밀한 수치가 필요하면 숫자를 쓰게 하지 말고 vocab을 확장한다. Table 5는 이것 하나로 PDMS +9.23점, 런타임 −48%를 얻었다. 텍스트에서 float를 파싱하는 태스크는 모두 후보다.

One more, on the data side: the reasoning-annotation trick — give the teacher the ground-truth answer and ask it to justify that — is the cheapest quality lever in the paper and has nothing to do with driving. Any time you distil explanations from a big model, rationalising a known label beats predicting an unknown one.

선생님에게 정답을 주고 정당화하게 하는 주석 트릭은 가장 싼 품질 레버이고 자율주행과 무관하다. 설명을 증류할 때는 예측보다 합리화가 낫다.
