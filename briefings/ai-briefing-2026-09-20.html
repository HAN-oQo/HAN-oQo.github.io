ProblemAutoregressive models generate one token at a time, left to right. Masked diffusion models decode in parallel but cannot reuse a KV cache, and their output loses coherence.자기회귀 모델은 한 토큰씩 순서대로 생성한다. 마스크드 확산 모델은 병렬 디코드가 되지만 KV 캐시를 재사용하지 못하고 출력 일관성도 떨어진다.

MethodOne architecture optimizes an AR objective and a masked-diffusion objective together, with a curriculum that grows slot size to move from fine-grained AR toward coarse-grained diffusion learning. ProblemDiffusion LLMs decode in parallel by iteratively unmasking, but they typically need many steps before a token's confidence crosses the decoding threshold. Even with block-wise KV caching, that makes inference wasteful.확산 LLM은 병렬로 언마스킹하지만 토큰 확신도가 임계값을 넘기까지 스텝이 많이 필요해, 블록 단위 KV 캐싱을 써도 낭비가 크다.

MethodTokens with similarly low entropy cluster together and can be decoded jointly before reaching the threshold.
