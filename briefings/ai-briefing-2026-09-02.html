vLLM — the KV connector learns to share a cache between mismatched tensor-parallel layouts Sep 1

The day's centre of gravity was the KV-connector layer, and specifically the Mooncake store. One change lets a producer and a consumer with different tensor-parallel degrees share the same stored cache — the case that blocks you from prefilling on an 8-way instance and decoding on a 2-way one. Three bugfixes cluster around it: a wrong physical-block transfer length in Mooncake, producer partial tails that were never offloaded when a request finished, and a DecodeBench block-selection error under decode context parallelism. Away from the connector, a ModelOpt refactor rebuilt the LinearMethod classes on the generic QuantKey-driven method, and the Kimi-K3 work continued with router-weight prefetching for the M=1 case. Mooncake 스토어 커넥터가 TP 차수가 다른 생산자와 소비자 사이에서 같은 캐시를 공유하게 됐다. 8-way에서 프리필하고 2-way에서 디코드하는 구성이 가능해진다. 물리 블록 전송 길이 오류, 요청 종료 시 생산자 부분 꼬리 미오프로드, DCP에서의 DecodeBench 블록 선택 오류도 고쳤다. 그 밖에 ModelOpt LinearMethod가 QuantKey 기반으로 재작성됐고, Kimi-K3에는 M=1 라우터 가중치 프리페치가 들어갔다.

SGLang — deleting host–device syncs from the sliding-window path, and sizing the graph pool from measurements Sep 1

Faithfulness Is Not Free: Auditing Offline KV-Cache Quantization in Retrieval-Augmented GenerationKV cachequantizationarXiv:2608.30996
arxiv.org/abs/2608.30996 →
Problem문제 정의A RAG system can precompute the KV cache of each retrieved document once and reuse it, so the context never has to be re-encoded per query. Quantizing those stored caches shrinks the storage bill further. The paper's point is that nobody has asked what compression does to faithfulness — whether the answer is still supported by the evidence the model was given — as opposed to accuracy.RAG는 문서 KV 캐시를 미리 계산해 재사용하고, 이를 양자화하면 저장 비용이 더 준다. 그런데 압축이 정확도가 아닌 충실성(답이 주어진 근거에 기반하는지)에 미치는 영향은 아무도 재지 않았다.
Method방법They evaluate Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with its offline caches quantized to INT8 and INT4, on RGB and HotpotQA. Accuracy is measured as usual; faithfulness is measured three separate ways — a hallucination detector, NLI entailment against the retrieved passages, and an LLM judge — so that a single flaky metric cannot carry the conclusion.Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct의 오프라인 캐시를 INT8·INT4로 양자화해 RGB와 HotpotQA에서 평가한다. 충실성은 환각 탐지기, NLI 함의, LLM 심판 세 가지로 따로 잰다.
Results결과INT8 is near-lossless on both accuracy and faithfulness. INT4 lowers accuracy, and — the more interesting finding — among the answers that stay factually correct, over 90% of the faithfulness changes are negative. Accuracy metrics are simply blind to that regression. The harm grows under noisy retrieval and as more chunks are retrieved.INT8은 거의 무손실이다. INT4는 정확도가 떨어지고, 정답으로 남은 답에서도 충실성 변화의 90% 이상이 부정적이다. 정확도 지표로는 보이지 않으며, 검색 잡음과 청크 수가 늘수록 나빠진다.
Summary요약The practical rule the paper hands you is short: INT8 offline caches are safe, INT4 is not, and "accuracy held" is not evidence that compression was harmless. Faithfulness has to be audited separately before a compressed cache goes into production.INT8 오프라인 캐시는 안전하고 INT4는 아니다. 정확도 유지가 무해하다는 증거는 아니므로 충실성은 따로 점검해야 한다.

A Universal Context-Reuse Layer for Cross-Model KV Sharingprefix cachingheterogeneous servingarXiv:2608.30963
arxiv.org/abs/2608.30963 →
Problem문제 정의Serving stacks reuse KV caches aggressively, but every existing mechanism assumes the producer and the consumer of a cache are the same model. In a fleet running several models over the same shared context — a document, a system prompt, a conversation history — each model still pays its own full prefill for text another model has already read.기존 KV 재사용은 생산자와 소비자가 같은 모델이라고 전제한다. 그래서 같은 문맥을 여러 모델이 읽으면 모델마다 프리필을 다시 한다.
Method방법The paper studies cross-model KV sharing: translating the KV state a source model produced into a representation a different target model can consume. The target may differ in scale, architecture, attention configuration, tokenizer and model family. They call the resulting abstraction "context mobility" — treating KV state as a transferable computational representation rather than something strictly local to one model.소스 모델의 KV 상태를 규모·구조·토크나이저·계열이 다른 타깃 모델이 쓸 수 있는 표현으로 변환한다. 저자들은 이를 "문맥 이동성(context mobility)"이라 부른다.
Results결과Cross-family handoff from Qwen2.5-1.5B to Gemma-2-2B cuts target-side prefill cost by up to 67.05% at 4K context while keeping decoding perplexity close to native. Handing off from Llama3.1-70B to Qwen2.5-7B reaches 44.0% accuracy against 45.7% for native Qwen2.5-7B inference, with measured latency dropping from 899ms to 138ms. A handoff into a 1.5B target also lifts LongBench2 accuracy from 27.59% to 34.48%.Qwen2.5-1.5B → Gemma-2-2B 인계는 4K에서 타깃 프리필 비용을 최대 67.05% 줄인다. Summary요약The authors present this as initial evidence rather than a finished system, but the framing matters for anyone running a heterogeneous fleet or a multi-agent workflow: if KV state is portable, the natural unit of caching stops being (model, prefix) and becomes just (prefix), with a translation layer in between.아직 초기 증거다. 다만 KV가 옮겨진다면 캐시 키는 (모델, 프리픽스)가 아니라 (프리픽스)가 된다.

Q-Strata: Hierarchical Bit Allocation for Mixed-Precision Quantization of Mixture-of-Experts LLMsquantizationMoEarXiv:2608.30564
arxiv.org/abs/2608.30564 →
Problem문제 정의Mixed-precision quantization gives each linear layer its own bitwidth so that a fixed budget buys the least quality loss. In an MoE model those layers exist in every expert of every block, so the allocation space explodes. Existing approaches either fix a uniform per-block budget and allocate inside it, or allocate across blocks through an additive proxy — and neither optimizes a model-level objective over the choices that actually couple the blocks together.MoE에서는 선형 계층이 모든 전문가에 있어 혼합 정밀도 비트 배분 공간이 폭발한다. 기존 방법은 블록 예산을 고정하거나 덧셈형 대리 지표를 써서, 블록 간 결합을 반영한 모델 수준 목적함수를 최적화하지 못한다.
Method방법Q-Strata is a bi-level allocator. The inner stage ranks within-block assignments with a cheap proxy and caches a Pareto frontier of candidates per block over finely spaced budgets. That leaves the outer stage with a much smaller job: set one budget per block, rather than a bitwidth for every linear layer. With the search collapsed that far, the outer stage can evaluate the model-level objective directly on the assembled quantized model, which is where the inter-block coupling lives.2단 배분기다. 내부 단계는 값싼 대리 지표로 블록별 파레토 프론티어를 캐싱하고, 외부 단계는 블록당 예산 하나만 고르면서 조립된 모델의 실제 목적함수를 평가한다.
Results결과On Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct, Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B and DeepSeek-V2-Lite, Q-Strata consistently reaches lower WikiText2 perplexity than uniform-bitwidth GPTQ and than the MoE mixed-precision methods MxMoE and GEMQ, in the low-bit regime where the differences matter. The code is released.Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct, Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B, DeepSeek-V2-Lite의 저비트 구간에서 GPTQ, MxMoE, GEMQ보다 WikiText2 퍼플렉시티가 낮다. 코드가 공개돼 있다.
Summary요약The idea generalizes past MoE: when a search space is too large to optimize the real objective on, compress the space with a cheap proxy first, then spend the expensive objective on the small remainder. Here that means proxy-ranking inside blocks and a true model-level evaluation across them.탐색 공간이 크면 값싼 대리 지표로 먼저 줄이고, 남은 후보에만 비싼 목적함수를 쓴다.

Ceiling-Clipped Acceptance Histograms Indicate Stranded Speed-up in Block-Diffusion Speculative Decodingspeculative decodingdiffusion LLMarXiv:2608.30427
arxiv.org/abs/2608.30427 →
Problem문제 정의Block-diffusion drafters such as DFlash and DFlare fill a whole block in one parallel pass, and they are good enough that the target frequently accepts the entire block. When that happens the drafter has run out of trained block horizon before verification ever failed — speed-up that was available but never collected. The paper names this stranded speed-up, and points out that a mean committed length hides it completely.DFlash·DFlare 같은 블록 디퓨전 드래프터는 블록 전체가 수용되는 일이 잦다. 그만큼 더 얻을 수 있던 속도가 남는데, 논문은 이를 stranded speed-up이라 부른다. 평균 커밋 길이로는 보이지 않는다.
Method방법The diagnostic is the acceptance histogram rather than its mean: a spike in the ceiling bin — the fraction of cycles that accept the whole block — is the signature. The authors recommend it as a preflight check before spending training compute. The fix is not to widen the block at inference time, because once the block outgrows its training size the drafter's bidirectional attention shifts its distribution even at early positions and erodes front-of-block verification. Instead they post-train the drafter on a longer block with a short curriculum emphasising the newly exposed positions — a method they call DBloom.수용률 히스토그램의 천장 구간에 봉우리가 서면 신호다. 추론 때 블록만 넓히면 양방향 어텐션 탓에 앞쪽 검증이 망가지므로, 새 위치를 강조하는 짧은 커리큘럼으로 사후 학습한다(DBloom).
Results결과Expanding pretrained DFlash and DFlare drafters from block size 16 to 24, against Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-4B targets, raises per-prompt committed length on the high-ceiling benchmarks by a median of +0.8 tokens (up to +1.1). With continuation fine-tuning before expansion, the increase reaches 1.37 tokens. The same expansion lifts committed length on all seven benchmarks for Gemma-4-12B-IT, a different model family, by a median of +0.41.블록 크기를 16에서 24로 늘려 Qwen3-8B·Qwen3-4B에 붙이면 커밋 길이가 중앙값 +0.8토큰(최대 +1.1) 늘고, 연속 파인튜닝을 먼저 하면 1.37토큰까지 는다. Gemma-4-12B-IT에서도 일곱 벤치마크 모두 중앙값 +0.41이다.
Summary요약The takeaway is diagnostic before it is methodological: if your acceptance histogram piles up against the ceiling, the drafter is not the bottleneck — the block size is, and you can tell before committing training compute to a better drafter.히스토그램이 천장에 몰려 있으면 병목은 드래프터가 아니라 블록 크기다. 학습 전에 확인할 수 있다.
