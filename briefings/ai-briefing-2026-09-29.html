Problem문제 정의Multimodal LLMs add an Encode phase in front of Prefill and Decode, and existing frameworks either lack it (text-only PD systems) or expose it as a standalone service that does not regulate downstream flow. Every request enters through Encode, yet per-request execution leaves the encode GPU badly underused even at high load, starving the Prefill and Decode workers behind it.멀티모달 LLM은 프리필 앞에 인코드 단계가 붙지만, 기존 프레임워크는 이를 다루지 않거나 흐름을 조절하지 않는 독립 서비스로만 둔다. 요청 단위 실행은 고부하에서도 인코드 GPU를 놀려 뒤의 프리필·디코드 워커를 굶긴다.

Method방법EAServe treats Encode as the control point of the Encode-Prefill-Decode pipeline. Its runtime does load-adaptive micro-batching, rate-controlled partial offload of prefill to a co-resident worker, and dynamic SM partitioning for predictable co-location; a configuration layer (Hybrid Auto Selection) prunes unbalanced GPU allocations with per-stage capacity profiling and refines the rest with TPE-based Bayesian optimization.인코드를 파이프라인의 제어 지점으로 삼아 부하 적응형 마이크로 배칭, 속도 제어형 부분 프리필 오프로드, 동적 SM 분할을 한다. 구성 계층(Hybrid Auto Selection)은 단계별 프로파일링으로 불균형 할당을 쳐내고 TPE 베이지안 최적화로 나머지를 다듬는다.

Results결과On three MLLM architectures spanning image, video and audio, EAServe delivers up to 4.3x and 1.7x higher goodput than NVIDIA Dynamo and vLLM under identical SLO constraints, with more balanced GPU utilization and faster convergence to near-optimal configurations. Problem문제 정의Lossy speculative decoding allows the output to drift from the target model to gain speed, and has been observed to sometimes even beat the target on quality, but there was no formal account of how that can happen.손실 추측 디코딩은 속도를 위해 타깃 모델에서 벗어나는 출력을 허용하고 때로 품질이 타깃을 넘지만, 그 이유에 대한 형식적 설명이 없었다.

Method방법The paper formalizes lossy speculative decoding as mentored decoding and connects inference to boosting theory, generalizing to all f-divergences.
