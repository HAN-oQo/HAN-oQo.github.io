EU Commission presents an Action Plan on Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence Jul 7

The European Commission published an Action Plan for a structured response to the risks and opportunities that advanced AI models pose for cybersecurity, warning that AI can be misused to find vulnerabilities and automate attacks at unprecedented scale and speed. Building on the EU's existing AI and cybersecurity legal framework, the plan aims to bring together Member States, industry and EU-level bodies to harden the digital landscape. It is a policy/coordination move rather than new regulation, backed by a factsheet and the full press release.
