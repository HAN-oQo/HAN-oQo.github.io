Problem문제 정의A model with a bounded KV cache must repeatedly decide which stored items to keep. Every deployed method (StreamingLLM, H2O, SnapKV) commits the moment an item arrives — either from the past or from a guess about the future — whereas Belady's offline optimum needs the whole future.KV 캐시가 제한되면 무엇을 남길지 계속 정해야 한다. StreamingLLM·H2O·SnapKV는 항목이 들어오는 순간 결정하고, Belady 최적해는 미래 전체를 알아야 한다.
Method방법The authors recast eviction as estimating a hidden "will-be-reused" signal, indexed by a commit lag H. The missing middle regime — fixed-lag smoothing — waits a bounded number of steps, observes which items a correct near-future prediction attended to, then commits. They instantiate it as a training-free policy, RMM, a strict generalization of H2O.축출을 커밋 지연 H를 둔 "재사용 여부" 추정 문제로 본다. 그 중간 영역인 고정 지연 평활(fixed-lag smoothing)은 몇 스텝 기다려 맞힌 예측이 어디에 어텐션했는지 본 뒤 결정한다. 이를 학습 없는 정책 RMM(H2O의 일반화)으로 구현했다.
Results결과When reuse is endogenous and time-separated, "demonstrated utility" identifies used memory far better than accumulated attention. But on third-party KVPress benchmarks the advantage mostly vanishes: RMM ties H2O on single-turn QA and loses to both H2O and SnapKV in streaming multi-turn — because on natural text the model is right about most tokens, so weighting by correctness barely changes anything. The contribution is the framework and an honest map of where it helps.재사용이 내생적이고 시간상 떨어져 있으면 "입증된 유용성"이 누적 어텐션보다 훨씬 정확하다. 하지만 KVPress 벤치마크에선 단일턴 QA에서 H2O와 비기고 스트리밍 다중턴에선 H2O·SnapKV에 진다. 자연어에선 모델이 대부분 토큰을 맞혀서 정확도 가중의 효과가 작다.
Summary요약Frame KV eviction as estimation with a commit lag; fixed-lag smoothing helps only when reuse is sharp and endogenous — an honest negative on standard benchmarks.KV 축출을 커밋 지연이 있는 추정 문제로 정리했다. 재사용이 뚜렷할 때만 이득이 있고 표준 벤치마크에선 이기지 못한다고 솔직히 보고한다.

Problem문제 정의Serving LLMs at long context is bottlenecked by the KV cache, which is read in full at every decode step. Attention keys are locally low-rank but globally high-rank, so shared low-rank bases throw away the page-specific directions that a page's own basis would keep.롱컨텍스트 디코딩은 매 스텝 KV 캐시 전체를 읽느라 느리다. 키는 국소적으론 저랭크지만 전역적으론 고랭크라서, 공유 저랭크 기저를 쓰면 페이지 고유 방향을 잃는다.
Method방법LOCKS gives every page its own resident spectral summary (about a tenth of the cache's size), reconstructs within-page logits from it, estimates each page's attention mass via log-sum-exp, and attends only the top pages — selection itself reads no candidate keys or values.페이지마다 스펙트럼 요약(캐시의 약 1/10)을 두고, log-sum-exp로 페이지별 어텐션 질량을 추정해 상위 페이지만 어텐션한다. 선택 단계에선 키·값을 읽지 않는다.
Results결과Selecting on the summary alone stays within about a point of the full cache on LongBench-v1, tracks a read-every-key oracle on RULER down to tiny budgets, and widens its margin on long-form reasoning (AIME26, MATH-500). At a 2048-token budget it matches FullKV quality at 100K+ context while attending about 2% of the tokens, and halves per-token decode latency (2.0× at 1M tokens). Problem문제 정의Modern LLM systems produce rich structured priors (ranked evidence, graph topology, confidence signals), but once serialized into a prompt the backend sees only a flat token sequence and consumes the full KV state uniformly. Kimi K3's "open" weights come with a revenue-tiered license Jul 27

The Kimi K3 License reads like MIT for most of its length, but two conditions attach above a revenue line. A "Model as a Service" business must sign a separate agreement with Moonshot once revenue passes $20M over any 12 months, and any product with more than 100M monthly active users or over $20M monthly revenue must display "Kimi K3" prominently in its interface. Neither applies to purely internal use — so the split is by business model, not by user: a team fine-tuning K3 on its own infrastructure carries no obligation, while a cloud provider reselling K3 inference at scale needs a contract.
