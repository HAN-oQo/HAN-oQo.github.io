GitLost — tricking a GitHub AI agent into leaking private repositories Jul 9

Noma Labs disclosed GitLost, an indirect-prompt-injection flaw in GitHub's new Agentic Workflows: an unauthenticated attacker who merely opens a crafted issue in a public repo could steer the agent into exfiltrating a private repo's contents into a publicly-visible issue comment.
