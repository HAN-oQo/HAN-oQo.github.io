Method방법MISA-T is a routing-layer admission policy for mixed rollout serving. Problem문제 정의Standard round-to-nearest (RTN) post-training quantization has an inherent ambiguity for weights sitting near the center of a quantization interval: rounding up or down is nearly a coin-flip, yet the choice materially affects a low-bit model. Calibration-based fixes need data and extra compute, which is exactly what calibration-free deployment wants to avoid.반올림(RTN) 양자화에서 구간 중앙 근처 가중치는 올림과 내림이 거의 동전 던지기인데, 저비트에선 이 선택이 품질을 좌우한다. 보정 기반 해법은 데이터와 연산이 더 든다.
Method방법ReRound trains a conditional diffusion model to produce continuous reconstructions of a pretrained LLM's low-bit weights. Summary요약ReRound's insight is that most RTN error at low bits concentrates near interval midpoints, and a diffusion reconstruction can resolve exactly those ties — no calibration data required.저비트 RTN 오차는 중점 근처에 몰리고, 확산 재구성이 그 동점을 보정 데이터 없이 풀어 준다.

Problem문제 정의RL for LLMs is moving to multi-turn agentic workloads where rollout tasks repeatedly pause for external environments, resume with growing contexts, and finish at highly variable times. Method방법TideRL is a readiness-aware elastic RL system with three parts: Continuous Task Batching (CTB) preserves useful rollout state across pauses; Resource-Aware Ref-Actor Pipelining (RA²P) selects between decoupled streaming and colocated aggregation based on the ready backlog and arrival interval; and Elastic Resource Scaling (ERS) moves GPU ranks between rollout and training as demand shifts.TideRL은 세 부분으로 되어 있다. Results결과By keeping rollout state warm, matching the pipelining mode to backlog and arrivals, and reallocating ranks elastically, TideRL cuts the GPU idling and prefill recomputation that dominate variable-length agentic rollouts, raising training goodput under the same hardware.GPU 유휴와 프리필 재계산을 줄여 같은 하드웨어에서 학습 goodput을 높인다.
