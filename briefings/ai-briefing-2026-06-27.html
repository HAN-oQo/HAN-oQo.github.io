Problem문제 정의On Apple Silicon, the fp32 GEMMs that dominate LLM prefill are dispatched by Accelerate to the AMX matrix coprocessor. Method방법Microbenchmarks show the inner loop is load-issue bound — once operand loads interleave with the FMA32 stream, single-thread throughput falls to a 610–680 GFLOPS band.
