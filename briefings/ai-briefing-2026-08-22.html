Learning how to Forget: Fine-tuning for Long-Context Sparse Attentionsparse attentionKV-cache2608.19920
arXiv:2608.19920 — Learning how to Forget: Fine-tuning for Long-Context Sparse Attention
Problem문제 정의A large body of prior work makes long-context inference affordable by selecting or compressing the KV cache with sparse attention, but the model is normally trained with exact attention and only meets the sparsity policy at inference time. That mismatch means the model never learns to route information around whatever the policy throws away. The paper asks whether the model can instead be fine-tuned so that it co-adapts with the policy, and whether that can be done without a large training cluster.희소 어텐션으로 KV 캐시를 줄이는 연구는 많지만, 모델은 정확한 어텐션으로 학습하고 추론 때 처음 희소 정책을 만난다. 논문은 모델을 정책과 함께 파인튜닝할 수 있는지, 그것도 대형 클러스터 없이 가능한지를 묻는다.
Method방법The authors present a fine-tuning method for models with sparse attention that is policy-agnostic: it works for any KV-cache policy rather than being tied to one selection rule. The training path is designed for a moderate hardware budget — the paper names a single 40 GB NVIDIA A100 as sufficient — so the co-adaptation loop is reachable without sequence-parallel infrastructure. Alongside the method they contribute an efficient implementation of H2O sparse attention, the strongest policy in their experiments, with dedicated scaled-dot-product-attention kernel support.특정 선택 규칙에 묶이지 않는 정책 불특정 파인튜닝 방법을 제안하며, 40GB A100 한 장이면 충분하다. 실험에서 가장 강했던 H2O의 효율적 구현(전용 SDPA 커널 포함)도 함께 내놓는다.
Results결과The headline claim is that models fine-tuned this way often outperform models trained with exact attention under sequence parallelism — that is, letting the model adapt to a lossy policy can beat training it on the full attention it will not have at serving time. H2O emerges as the leading policy among those tested. The work ships as KeysAndValues, a new open-source library for long-context inference and fine-tuning covering all the methods discussed.이렇게 파인튜닝한 모델이 시퀀스 병렬로 정확한 어텐션 학습을 한 모델보다 자주 낫고, 정책 중에서는 H2O가 가장 좋았다. 코드는 오픈소스 라이브러리 KeysAndValues로 공개됐다.
Summary요약The interesting inversion here is that sparse attention stops being purely an inference-time compromise and becomes part of the training objective. If the policy is fixed at serving time anyway, training under it is the honest thing to do — and the single-A100 budget is what makes that argument practical rather than aspirational. The policy-agnostic framing matters for serving teams because it means the method survives a change of KV-cache policy.서빙 때 정책이 어차피 고정된다면 그 정책으로 학습하는 편이 맞다. CacheRoute: Planned Prefix-Affinity Routing for Large-Scale LLM ServingKV-cacherouting2608.19677
arXiv:2608.19677 — CacheRoute: Planned Prefix-Affinity Routing for Large-Scale LLM Serving
Problem문제 정의Prefix caching only saves prefill work if a repeated request lands back on the server that still holds that prefix's KV. A cache-blind load balancer spreads those repeats across the fleet and destroys the reuse; fixed affinity preserves the reuse but concentrates traffic and can overload a server. The paper frames this as a genuine tradeoff rather than a solved routing problem, because both failure modes are expensive in different currencies.프리픽스 캐싱은 반복 요청이 KV를 가진 서버로 돌아갈 때만 이득이다. 캐시를 모르는 분산은 재사용을 깨고, 고정 어피니티는 트래픽을 몰아 과부하를 만든다.
Method방법CacheRoute resolves the tradeoff with a periodic routing plan rather than a per-request decision. It admits high-rate prefix keys into a stable warm set, then places their assignments according to expected load, so affinity is granted where it pays and load is respected where it does not. Hot keys are allowed more than one destination, although the authors report that in their primary semi-synthetic aggregate every key ended up using exactly one — a detail worth noting because it means the multi-destination machinery was available but not load-bearing there.CacheRoute는 요청 단위가 아니라 주기적 계획으로 라우팅한다. 요청률이 높은 프리픽스 키만 warm set에 넣고 기대 부하에 맞춰 배치하며, 다중 목적지도 지원하지만 주요 집계에서는 모든 키가 목적지 하나만 썼다.
Results결과On Llama-3.3-70B in fp8 across 60 H100 GPUs, CacheRoute sustains 176±11 QPS at a 3.5-second p99 SLO, which is 2.3× the strongest of five baselines. Served KV-cache hit rate rises from 64.1±1.3% under cache-blind balancing to 93.2±0.5%. The paper is unusually forthright about where it fails: two 32B workloads are presented as counterexamples where affinity recovers too little KV work, and its residual load skew reduces or entirely erases the improvement. The authors' own recommendation follows from that — gate any deployment with a shadow replay rather than turning affinity on from workload statistics alone.H100 60장, fp8 Llama-3.3-70B에서 p99 3.5초 SLO 기준 176±11 QPS로 가장 강한 기준선(5종 중)의 2.3배였고, KV 히트율은 64.1±1.3%에서 93.2±0.5%로 올랐다. 다만 32B 워크로드 2건에서는 회수되는 KV가 적어 이득이 줄거나 사라졌고, 저자들은 섀도 리플레이로 배포를 판단하라고 권한다.
Summary요약The useful takeaway is a decision rule, not a router: prefix affinity is worth a large multiple when the recovered KV work exceeds the load skew it introduces, and worth negative when it does not, and the crossover depends on the workload rather than on the algorithm. That is why the paper's shadow-replay recommendation is the actionable part for anyone running a KV-aware router today. The 64% → 93% hit-rate jump is also a reminder of how much reuse a cache-blind balancer quietly discards.어피니티는 회수되는 KV 작업이 부하 편중보다 클 때만 이득이며, 그 경계는 워크로드가 정한다. 64%에서 93%로 오른 히트율은 균형 배분이 재사용을 얼마나 버리는지 보여 준다.

arXiv:2608.19659 — FleetSieve: Decision-Critical Profiling for SLO-Aware LLM Fleet Configuration
Problem문제 정의Picking tensor-parallel degree and replica count for a serving fleet is hard for a specific reason: performance is not monotonic in TP, and which configuration is feasible can change with load. Exhaustive profiling removes the uncertainty but spends GPU-hours measuring configurations that will not change the final allocation.
