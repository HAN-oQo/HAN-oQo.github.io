HOLA — A Hippocampus for Linear Attention: An Exact Memory for What the Recurrent State Forgetslinear attentionlong context2607.02303
HOLA (arXiv 2607.02303, submitted Jul 2) ↗HOLA (arXiv 2607.02303, 7/2 제출) ↗
Problem문제 정의Linear-attention and state-space models compress the prefix into a fixed-size recurrent state, giving O(1) memory but a lossy one: when many key–value associations compete, earlier facts get overwritten and needle recall degrades. There is no channel to keep the specific associations the state cannot hold.선형 어텐션·SSM은 프리픽스를 고정 크기 상태로 압축해 O(1) 메모리를 얻는다. 대신 연관이 많아지면 앞선 사실이 덮어써져 니들 회수가 나빠진다.
Method방법HOLA keeps the usual delta-rule state as compressive memory and adds a bounded exact KV cache — a semiparametric test-time memory. The cache writes without a learned eviction module, keeping tokens with large β·‖e‖ (the prediction residual actually committed to the state); a decoupled RMSNorm-γ cache read turns those exact pairs into sharp retrieval rather than soft averaging.delta-rule 상태는 그대로 두고, 경계 있는 정확 KV 캐시를 더한다. 학습된 축출 없이 β·‖e‖(상태에 커밋된 예측 잔차)가 큰 토큰을 남기고, RMSNorm-γ 읽기로 평균이 아닌 정확한 회수를 한다.
Results결과At 340M parameters on 15B SlimPajama tokens, HOLA lowers Wikitext perplexity 27.32→22.92 (−16.1%), below a full-attention Transformer++ (26.88), and improves LAMBADA (30.95→30.26). It achieves the best linear in-context retrieval and stays robust on RULER needle-in-a-haystack out to 32k tokens — 16× its training length.340M·15B 토큰에서 Wikitext perplexity 27.32→22.92(−16.1%)로 Transformer++(26.88)보다 낮다. RULER needle-in-a-haystack에서 학습 길이의 16배인 32k까지 견고하다.
Summary요약Give linear attention a small exact-memory "hippocampus" so it stops forgetting the needles its compressive recurrent state cannot hold.선형 어텐션에 작은 정확 메모리 "해마"를 붙여 니들 망각을 막는다.

Load-Aware Prefill Deflection for Disaggregated LLM ServingPD disaggregationscheduling2607.02043
Prefill Deflection (arXiv 2607.02043, submitted Jul 2) ↗Prefill Deflection (arXiv 2607.02043, 7/2 제출) ↗
Problem문제 정의Disaggregated serving splits prefill and decode onto separate GPU pools to stop the phases interfering, but under bursty, heavy-tailed traffic prefill nodes saturate while decode nodes idle. On a 2P2D setup the authors find prefill execution is only 2–23% of P95 Time-to-First-Token (TTFT); queuing and inter-node GPU–GPU KV-cache transfer account for the rest.분리 서빙에서 버스티 트래픽이 오면 프리필 노드는 포화되고 디코드 노드는 논다. 2P2D에서 프리필 실행은 P95 TTFT의 2–23%뿐이고 나머지는 큐잉과 노드 간 KV 전송이다.
Method방법A proactive prefill-deflecting scheduler lets decode nodes serve a request's prefill as chunked-prefill steps interleaved with their in-flight decode batches. For each queued request it estimates the TTFT on the prefill node and searches each decode node for the largest chunk schedule that keeps in-flight decodes within their Time-Between-Tokens SLO, deflecting only when the decode path helps tail latency. Because deflected prefill runs in place, the inter-node KV transfer is eliminated.유휴 디코드 노드가 프리필을 청크로 쪼개 디코드 배치 사이에 끼워 처리한다. TBT SLO를 지키는 범위에서 꼬리 지연에 도움될 때만 넘기며, 제자리 실행이라 KV 전송이 사라진다.
Results결과Implemented on vLLM and evaluated on production-style traces with DeepSeek-V2-Lite, the approach reduces P95 TTFT by up to 81% and raises SLO attainment by up to 79% over state-of-the-art disaggregated schedulers, at sub-millisecond scheduling overhead.vLLM 구현, DeepSeek-V2-Lite 트레이스에서 P95 TTFT 최대 81% 감소, SLO 달성 최대 79% 향상, 오버헤드는 서브밀리초다.
Summary요약When decode GPUs sit idle, run the prefill there in chunks — eliminating the network KV transfer that actually dominates tail TTFT.놀고 있는 디코드 GPU에서 프리필을 돌려 꼬리 TTFT를 지배하는 KV 전송을 없앤다.

Spec-AUF — Accept-Until-Fail Training under Train-Inference Misalignment for Masked Block Draftersspeculative decodingtraining2607.01893
Spec-AUF (arXiv 2607.01893, submitted Jul 2) ↗Spec-AUF (arXiv 2607.01893, 7/2 제출) ↗
Problem문제 정의Block (DLM-style) drafters predict a whole block of tokens in parallel — fast, but trained with a full-block cross-entropy that supervises every position against the gold continuation, even though inference commits only the longest accepted prefix and discards every token after the first rejection. That is a train-inference mismatch.블록 드래프터는 추론 때 첫 거부 이전 프리픽스만 쓰는데, 학습은 블록 전체 위치에 손실을 건다. 학습과 추론이 어긋난다.
Method방법Accept-Until-Fail (AUF) concentrates supervision on the accepted prefix. A mask-only block drafter has no input-side channel for gold-prefix conditioning, so AUF approximates that prefix-sensitive supervision on the loss side by keeping the cross-entropy support only through the drafter's first predicted failure. It is a single detached change that touches neither the inference pipeline nor the exactness contract.AUF는 교차엔트로피를 드래프터의 첫 예측 실패 지점까지만 건다. 추론 파이프라인은 그대로 둔 단일 변경이다.
Results결과On Qwen3-8B, AUF raises the DFlash drafter's average emitted length τ (over six benchmarks) from 2.40→2.61, gaining on every benchmark, and transfers to Domino's two-branch head (2.56→2.68). Notably, once AUF truncates the support, the usual exponential position-decay weighting becomes empirically inert.Qwen3-8B에서 DFlash의 평균 방출 길이 τ가 2.40→2.61(6개 벤치마크 전부 개선), Domino 헤드도 2.56→2.68로 오른다. 지수 위치 감쇠 가중은 사실상 불필요해진다.
Summary요약Only supervise the tokens speculative decoding actually keeps — cut the loss at the drafter's first failure, not at the block's end.추측 디코딩이 실제로 남기는 토큰만 학습한다.

Lynx — Progressive Speculative Quantization for Accelerating KV Transfer in Long-Context InferenceKV transferlong context2607.01831
Lynx (arXiv 2607.01831, submitted Jul 2) ↗Lynx (arXiv 2607.01831, 7/2 제출) ↗
Problem문제 정의Long-context inference — driven by retrieval-augmented generation and agentic systems — is increasingly common in serving. In disaggregated inference these workloads transfer large KV caches across the network, and decoding cannot begin until the transfer completes, a hard latency wall.분리 추론에서 롱컨텍스트 요청은 큰 KV 캐시를 네트워크로 옮겨야 하고, 전송이 끝나야 디코딩을 시작할 수 있다.
Method방법Lynx challenges the assumption that a KV cache is an indivisible unit. Observing that different bits contribute unequally — most-significant bits carry the coarse attention structure, least-significant bits refine precision — it partitions the cache into a high-priority Anchor stream (MSBs) and a low-priority Residual stream. Decoding begins upon receiving the Anchor and proceeds speculatively while the Residual transfers concurrently, then verification guarantees equivalence to higher-precision decoding.비트마다 기여가 다르다는 점을 이용해 KV를 상위 비트 Anchor 스트림과 Residual 스트림으로 나눈다. Anchor가 오면 바로 추측 디코딩을 시작하고, Residual 도착 후 검증으로 고정밀 결과와 같음을 보장한다.
Results결과Across multiple models and serving workloads, Lynx reaches Time-to-First-Token comparable to aggressive 4-bit KV transfer while preserving higher-precision output quality through verification — i.e., progressive, split-stream KV transfer without the accuracy cost of static low-bit compression.여러 모델·워크로드에서 공격적 4-bit 전송 수준의 TTFT를 내면서 고정밀 품질을 유지한다.
Summary요약Send the KV cache's important bits first and start decoding speculatively, verifying against the remaining precision as it arrives.중요한 비트를 먼저 보내 디코딩을 시작하고 나머지로 검증한다.
Split KV into Anchor (MSBs) + Residual streams; decode on Anchor, verify on ResidualKV를 Anchor(MSB)와 Residual로 분할, Anchor로 디코드하고 Residual로 검증

PartRep (arXiv 2607.01792, submitted Jul 2) ↗PartRep (arXiv 2607.01792, 7/2 제출) ↗
Problem문제 정의Causal attention gives decoder-only LLMs an asymmetric information flow: later tokens are richer in context than earlier ones. Appending a second copy of the prompt redistributes grounding and improves reasoning, but full repetition doubles the KV-cache footprint and quadruples prefill attention cost — impractical for long contexts.인과 어텐션에선 뒤 토큰이 앞 토큰보다 맥락이 풍부하다. 프롬프트를 한 번 더 붙이면 추론이 좋아지지만 KV는 2배, 프리필 어텐션은 4배가 된다.
Method방법PartRep selectively appends only the most informative tokens rather than the entire prompt. It uses token-wise negative log-likelihood (NLL) as the selection signal, on the hypothesis that less-predictable (high-NLL) tokens are less recoverable from surrounding context and therefore benefit most from late-position repetition.예측하기 어려운(고-NLL) 토큰만 골라 뒤에 덧붙인다. 주변 맥락으로 복구가 어려운 토큰일수록 반복 이득이 크다는 가설이다.
Results결과PartRep recovers much of full repetition's reasoning gain at a fraction of the added KV/attention cost, making the grounding-redistribution trick affordable in long-context settings where full repetition is prohibitive.적은 추가 비용으로 전체 반복 이득의 상당 부분을 회복해 롱컨텍스트에서도 쓸 만하다.
Summary요약Repeat only the hard-to-predict (high-NLL) tokens late in the prompt — most of the benefit of prompt repetition, little of the cost.고-NLL 토큰만 반복해 이득은 대부분 얻고 비용은 줄인다.

3DLS — A 3D Logic-Stacked Architecture for Disaggregated LLM ServinghardwarePD disaggregation2607.01617
3DLS (arXiv 2607.01617, submitted Jul 2) ↗3DLS (arXiv 2607.01617, 7/2 제출) ↗

Method방법A difficulty-routed control architecture triggers "reconsideration" — concentrating deliberation and safeguards — before consequential backend writes, rather than applying additional control uniformly.
