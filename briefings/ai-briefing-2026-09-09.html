vLLM — sliding-window layers stop inflating the KV block LCM, and NIXL learns per-region transfer geometry Sep 8

The day filled a full API page of 100 commits, and the one with the widest blast radius is a paging change: SWA (sliding-window attention) layers now take the primary block size, so a hybrid model no longer forces the allocator to reconcile two block sizes through their least common multiple — an inflation that wastes cache and complicates every allocation path downstream. Alongside it, the NIXL KV connector gained per-region transfer geometry, the second step of a multi-part series, and attention backends must now declare DCP support explicitly rather than being assumed capable. Structured outputs got a real perf fix — the full-history reasoning scan on every step is gone — and NVFP4 saw three separate corrections: a fused SiLU-and-multiply scale allocation and global-scale direction bug, a KV speedup for FMHA, and a test that dequantizes KV cache scales in the layout the kernel actually writes. How to Speculate about Uncertainty in Agentic Coding? A Draft-Model Gate Methodspeculative decodinguncertaintyarXiv:2609.05274
arxiv.org/abs/2609.05274 →
Problem문제 정의Software-engineering agents fail expensively: they act confidently wrong, and the mistake only surfaces after the action has been executed and retried. The usual uncertainty signals are unavailable for a deployed black-box agent — no logits, no weights, no activations — and repeated sampling to estimate confidence multiplies the very cost you are trying to avoid.소프트웨어 엔지니어링 에이전트의 실패는 비싸다. 자신 있게 틀리고, 그 잘못은 행동을 실행하고 재시도한 뒤에야 드러난다. 배포된 블랙박스 에이전트에서는 흔히 쓰는 불확실성 신호를 얻을 수 없다. 로짓도, 가중치도, 활성도 없다. 신뢰도를 추정하려고 반복 샘플링을 하면 피하려던 비용이 그만큼 늘어난다.
Method방법Speculative Uncertainty inverts speculative decoding. Instead of a small draft model proposing tokens for a large model to verify, a small open-weight draft model scores the agent's already-generated trajectory in a single forward pass. The resulting cross-likelihoods are split into phase-aware features — reasoning spans separated from action spans — and calibrated against a verifiable objective, yielding a failure-likelihood score any downstream policy can consume. The paper instantiates one such policy, a pre-execution veto gate.Speculative Uncertainty는 스펙 디코딩을 뒤집는다. 작은 초안 모델이 토큰을 제안하고 큰 모델이 검증하는 대신, 작은 공개 가중치 초안 모델이 에이전트가 이미 생성한 궤적을 순전파 한 번으로 채점한다. 이렇게 얻은 교차 우도를 단계 인식 특징으로 나누고 — 추론 구간과 행동 구간을 분리한다 — 검증 가능한 목표에 맞춰 보정해, 하위 정책이 그대로 쓸 수 있는 실패 가능성 점수를 만든다. 논문은 그 정책 하나를 실행 전 거부 게이트로 구현한다.
Results결과On Qwen3-Coder-480B and the closed-source Claude 3.5 Sonnet, the veto gate cuts execution error rate by 6–8 percentage points and token cost by 14–19% in deployment. The signal transfers to out-of-distribution benchmarks without retraining and generalizes across agent models.Qwen3-Coder-480B와 상용 폐쇄 모델 Claude 3.5 Sonnet에서, 거부 게이트는 배포 환경의 실행 오류율을 6~8%p, 토큰 비용을 14~19% 줄인다. 이 신호는 재학습 없이 분포 밖 벤치마크로 옮겨 가고 에이전트 모델을 가리지 않는다.
Summary요약A neat reuse of infrastructure that serving stacks already have. Problem문제 정의Agents that plan over long horizons mostly select actions by unconstrained generation over an ever-growing history, which leaves the procedural knowledge — what to do, in what order, under which conditions — entirely implicit. Method방법The framing is a direct analogy: just as a knowledge graph organizes factual knowledge into (entity, relation, entity) triplets for what-is questions, a Procedural Graph organizes procedural knowledge into (procedure, relation, procedure) triplets for what-to-do questions.
