Method방법BlockLock keeps shared batched prefill and pins only the numerical path of the protected request by fixing its geometry: BlockLock-Rows uses fixed-width rows with batched decode, BlockLock-Pack uses terminal-anchored packing and extracts the KV state for target-only decode.BlockLock은 공유 배치 프리필을 유지하고 보호 대상 요청의 형상만 고정해 수치 경로를 고정한다.
