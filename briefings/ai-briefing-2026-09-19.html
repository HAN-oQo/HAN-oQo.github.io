SGLang v0.5.20 — the sampler starts handing the trainer its receipts, and the radix tree learns where requests fork Sep 18

A stable tag, 713 PRs from 237 contributors, plus 75 more commits merged in the window. The release's centre of gravity is the RL rollout loop. With return_sampling_mask, each decode step now returns the exact token support the sampler drew from and the log-probability of the sampled token under it, so a trainer can replay a rollout without reconstructing top-k or top-p from scratch — the reconstruction step that quietly makes off-policy corrections wrong. The masks run under overlap scheduling, which is what makes them affordable: on Qwen3-8B decode throughput is 17% higher at batch 1 and 52% higher at batch 64 than the previous implementation, with capacity set by --sampling-mask-max-tokens. The second structural change is branching-point caching for the SWA component of the unified radix tree: the sliding-window state is kept at the point where requests fork from a shared prefix, so branches reuse it instead of recomputing, lifting token hit rate from 43.8% to 60.8% on DeepSeek-V4-Flash with a shared system prompt and dropping mean TTFT from 1.57 s to 1.07 s. Around those, a CPU-only SGLang Simulator runs the real scheduler, radix cache and hierarchical cache with a latency predictor standing in for the forward pass, predicting TTFT within about 6% on most traces — a cache-and-scheduling research rig that needs no GPUs. Two removals matter more than they read: prefill context parallelism v1 is gone, leaving only the strategy-based path, and the CUDA 12 lane is retired, making v0.5.19 the last release with -cu12x wheels and images. Also in the window: a shortest-prefill-first scheduler, DCP allowed alongside decode radix cache and HiCache L1/L2, and optimistic prefill with buffer-only L3 write-through. 안정 태그이며 기여자 237명의 PR 713개, 여기에 구간 내 머지 커밋 75개가 더해졌다. 릴리스의 무게중심은 RL 롤아웃 루프다. return_sampling_mask로 각 디코드 스텝이 샘플러가 뽑아 쓴 정확한 토큰 집합과 그 아래에서의 샘플 토큰 로그 확률을 함께 돌려준다. 트레이너가 top-k나 top-p를 처음부터 재구성하지 않고 롤아웃을 재생할 수 있다는 뜻이고, 그 재구성 단계가 바로 오프폴리시 보정을 조용히 틀리게 만들던 곳이다. 마스크는 오버랩 스케줄링 아래에서 도는데, 그게 비용을 감당 가능하게 만든다. vLLM — KV-event publishers stop guessing their own port, and offloading learns to push back Sep 19

Fifty-eight commits in the window, no new tag. Two changes stand out for anyone running this in a cluster. First, KV-event publishers can now bind at port 0 and expose the endpoints they actually got, which removes a whole category of deployment breakage: until now a port had to be reserved in advance and agreed on by the consumer, and a collision surfaced as a silently missing event stream rather than a startup error. Three router commits landed within minutes of each other and read as one design: a request classifier seam, a deadline-aware policy queue ordering, and pluggable router flow control. On-Demand Attention: Language Models Know When to Recalllong contextdecodingarXiv:2609.20734
arxiv.org/abs/2609.20734 — On-Demand Attention: Language Models Know When to Recall

Problem Full-attention decoding reads the entire growing history at every step, whether or not that read helps the next token. For reasoning and agentic workloads, where context keeps accumulating, this is the dominant cost of long-context decoding. The usual fixes — fixed local windows, static sparsity patterns — decide in advance which history to ignore and then permanently lose whatever they discarded. 전체 어텐션 디코딩은 매 스텝마다 커져 가는 이력 전체를 읽는다. 그 읽기가 다음 토큰에 도움이 되든 말든 그렇다. 컨텍스트가 계속 쌓이는 추론·에이전트 워크로드에서는 이것이 장문맥 디코딩 비용의 지배적 항목이다. 흔한 해법 — 고정 지역 윈도, 정적 희소 패턴 — 은 어떤 이력을 무시할지 미리 정하고, 버린 것을 영구히 잃는다.

Method The authors show that a pretrained model's decoding state already contains information predictive of whether the global read will help — before that read happens. On-Demand Attention (ODA) is local-first: a lightweight recall head trained on top of frozen weights decides, step by step, whether to invoke global attention. Only the recall head is trained; the pretrained weights and the complete historical KV cache stay intact, so anything skipped at one step remains available at a later one. 저자들은 사전학습 모델의 디코딩 상태가 전역 읽기의 이득을 예측하는 정보를 이미 담고 있음을 — 그 읽기가 일어나기 전에 — 보인다. Anthropic opens a verification program that trades real-time blocking for offline monitoring in biology Sep 17

The Life Sciences Verification Program gives vetted life-science professionals access to Mythos, Opus and Sonnet with classifiers tuned to be more permissive for biology work that the generally available models block — drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, manufacturing. Applicants are reviewed on research credentials, security standards and ethical oversight, then granted either Standard Use, renewed yearly and extendable to a whole team, or High-risk Use, an add-on tied to a single project and renewed every six months. The structural change is in enforcement: for LSVP traffic, safeguards shift from rejecting individual requests in real time to offline monitoring of patterns across sessions, which is how serious misuse spread thinly across many requests is meant to be caught — and which requires 30-day retention of flagged activity, compartmentalized from training and from Anthropic's own life-science research teams.
