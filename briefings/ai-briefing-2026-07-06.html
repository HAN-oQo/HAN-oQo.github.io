Problem문제 정의State-of-the-art LLM training runs on tens of thousands of GPUs for months and hits failures across the software and hardware stack. Method방법DeadPool restores training via hot-swapping — replacing failed nodes with spare nodes without terminating the running job. Results결과DeadPool positions itself to hit low failure-free overhead and low recovery latency simultaneously — the two ends of the trade-off that prior mechanisms had to choose between — by hot-swapping spare nodes into a live job rather than restarting.DeadPool은 재시작 대신 예비 노드를 라이브 잡에 핫스왑함으로써, 기존 기법이 양자택일해야 했던 낮은 무장애 오버헤드와 낮은 복구 지연을 동시에 달성하도록 설계된다.
