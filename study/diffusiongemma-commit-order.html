Neither Parallel Nor Sequential — how DiffusionGemma actually commits tokens (paper note) 병렬도 순차도 아니다 — DiffusionGemma는 실제로 토큰을 어떤 순서로 확정하는가 (논문 정리)

Diffusion LLMs are sold as "parallel" decoders — but nobody measures the order a shipped checkpoint actually commits tokens. This inference-only interpretability study hooks DiffusionGemma's own sampler and watches. Finding: it's neither clean parallel nor clean left-to-right — a partial, granularity-dependent left-to-right bias, with big parallel commit batches and one regime (JSON) that's order-independent. A companion to the diffusion-LLM note. Diffusion LLM은 "병렬" 디코더로 홍보되지만, 출시된 체크포인트가 실제로 토큰을 어떤 순서로 확정하는지는 아무도 안 잰다. 이 추론-전용 해석 연구는 DiffusionGemma 자체 샘플러에 훅을 걸어 관찰한다. 결론: 깨끗한 병렬도, 깨끗한 좌→우도 아님 — 부분적이고 해상도(granularity) 의존적인 좌→우 편향, 큰 병렬 커밋 배치, 그리고 순서 무관한 한 영역(JSON). diffusion-LLM 노트의 짝.

1Problem — nobody measured a shipped dLLM's commit order문제 정의 — 출시된 dLLM의 커밋 순서를 잰 적이 없다

Diffusion LLMs denoise a masked canvas, "in principle committing many positions in parallel rather than strictly left to right" — that parallelism is the selling point (lower latency than AR). But the order a deployed checkpoint actually finalizes tokens in is an empirical property of the trained model + sampler, not a guarantee of the architecture — and it had gone unmeasured. The literature even disagrees: some say learned order trends left-to-right, others document structured "anchor-first" out-of-order generation. Diffusion LLM은 마스킹된 canvas를 denoise하며 "원칙적으로 엄격한 좌→우가 아니라 여러 위치를 병렬 확정"한다 — 그 병렬성이 셀링포인트(AR보다 낮은 지연). 그러나 배포된 체크포인트가 실제로 토큰을 확정하는 순서는 아키텍처의 보장이 아니라 학습된 모델 + 샘플러의 경험적 성질이고 — 측정된 적이 없었다. 문헌도 엇갈린다: 학습된 순서가 좌→우로 기운다는 쪽, 구조적 "anchor-first" 비순차 생성을 보고하는 쪽.

"the order in which a deployed checkpoint actually finalizes tokens, and whether that order is sequential, parallel, or something in between, is an empirical property of the trained model and its sampler, not a given of the architecture."

— §1, ¶1 · arXiv:2606.14620 §1

So the question is concrete: take one public checkpoint (google/diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it), instrument it, and ask — sequential, parallel, or in between? Plus two follow-ups: does the model's commit confidence predict correctness, and is its accuracy competitive with a matched autoregressive sibling? 그래서 질문은 구체적이다: 공개 체크포인트 하나(google/diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it)를 계측해 묻는다 — 순차인가 병렬인가 그 사이인가? 그리고 둘 더: 모델의 커밋 확신도가 정답을 예측하는가, 그 정확도가 짝이 되는 자기회귀 형제와 견줄 만한가?

2Method — hook the sampler, measure the order honestly해결 방법 — 샘플러에 훅, 순서를 정직하게 측정

Purely observational: a forward hook on EntropyBoundSampler.accept_canvas(...) records, for every position p, the first accept-call that commits it (commit(p)) and the per-position entropy of the committing logits. No weights or sampling decisions are changed. 순수 관찰: EntropyBoundSampler.accept_canvas(...)에 forward 훅을 걸어, 각 위치 p마다 그것을 확정한 첫 accept-call(commit(p))과 확정 logits의 위치별 엔트로피를 기록. 가중치·샘플링 결정 무수정.

"We wrap it with a forward hook that records, for every position p, the first accept-call index that commits p … together with the per-position entropy of the committing logits … No model weights or sampling decisions are modified; the hook is purely observational."

— §3 (Instrumentation) · arXiv:2606.14620 §3

The order metric, and the honesty controls순서 지표와 정직성 통제

order score = τ_b( commit(p) , p ) (tie-aware Kendall, content positions only)

Plain-language of the math: 수식 쉬운 설명:

  • Kendall τ_b — over all pairs of positions, the fraction that agree on ordering between "when committed" and "left-to-right position," minus those that disagree, with a correction for ties. +1 = strict left-to-right, 0 = order-independent, negative = right-to-left. The tie correction matters because many tokens commit in the same accept-call — they're simultaneous, not "out of order," and a naive metric would wrongly punish that. Kendall τ_b — 모든 위치 쌍에 대해, "언제 확정"과 "좌→우 위치"의 순서가 일치하는 비율에서 불일치를 빼고 동점을 보정. +1 = 엄격한 좌→우, 0 = 순서 무관, 음수 = 우→좌. 많은 토큰이 같은 accept-call에 확정되므로 동점 보정이 중요 — 동시이지 "순서가 틀린" 게 아닌데, 순진한 지표는 이를 잘못 벌한다.
  • Block-τ_b sweep — group positions into B-token bins (B ∈ {4,8,16,32,64}), give each bin its earliest commit, recompute τ_b. Coarsening the ruler mechanically hides within-bin disorder, so τ_b rises with B. The point isn't the value — it's the shape: smooth and monotone with no jump at 16, which kills the idea of a privileged "16-token architectural block." Block-τ_b 스윕 — 위치를 B토큰 bin(B ∈ {4,8,16,32,64})으로 묶고 bin마다 가장 이른 커밋을 주어 τ_b 재계산. 자를 거칠게 하면 bin 내부 무질서가 가려져 τ_b가 B에 따라 오른다. 핵심은 값이 아니라 모양: 매끄럽고 단조이며 16에서 점프 없음 → "특권적 16토큰 아키텍처 블록"이라는 생각을 깬다.
  • Block-sequential control — a synthetic process where commit time = position // 16 (a clean 16-block left-to-right, tied within blocks). Its τ_b (≈0.94–0.96) is what clean block-autoregression would score. The gap down to the real τ_b (0.43–0.60) measures genuine sub-block disorder — the paper's load-bearing comparison. Block-sequential 통제 — 커밋 시점 = position // 16인 합성 과정(깨끗한 16블록 좌→우, 블록 내 동점). 그 τ_b(≈0.94–0.96)가 깨끗한 block-AR이 낼 값. 실제 τ_b(0.43–0.60)까지의 격차가 진짜 sub-block 무질서를 잰다 — 논문의 핵심 비교.
  • Entropy-at-commit = Shannon entropy H = −Σ p log p (nats) over the 262K vocab at the committing call — a confidence reading directly comparable to the sampler's entropy_bound = 0.1. AUROC of −entropy → correct tests whether higher confidence ⇒ correct (computed within each regime; pooling across regimes is a trap — see Simpson's paradox below). 커밋시 엔트로피 = 확정 call에서 262K vocab에 대한 Shannon 엔트로피 H = −Σ p log p(nats) — 샘플러의 entropy_bound = 0.1과 직접 비교 가능한 확신도. −entropy → 정답의 AUROC는 높은 확신 ⇒ 정답인지 검정(영역 내에서 계산; 영역 간 풀링은 함정 — 아래 Simpson's paradox).

A large part of the paper's contribution is the measurement methodology itself — handling trailing-EOS padding, commit ties, commit non-monotonicity, block-size sensitivity, and Simpson's-paradox pooling — offered as "how decoding order should be measured." 논문 기여의 큰 부분은 측정 방법론 자체 — trailing-EOS 패딩, 커밋 동점, 커밋 비단조성, block-size 민감도, Simpson's-paradox 풀링 처리 — "디코딩 순서를 어떻게 측정해야 하는가"로 제시.

Confidence, early stopping, and accuracy확신도·조기 종료·정확도

Commit confidence predicts correctness on math (AUROC 0.749, CI excludes 0.5) but is a null on factual recall (0.471) — a regime-specific, not universal, signal. Pooling all regimes gives AUROC 0.437, which looks like "more confident ⇒ more often wrong" but is a Simpson's-paradox artifact, not a finding. The sampler is aggressive: generations finish in 3.3–17.1 accept-calls (vs the 48-step budget) at entropy 0.002–0.012 nats — one to two orders of magnitude below the 0.1 bound. And accuracy is comparable to the matched AR sibling gemma-4-26B-A4B-it (e.g. GSM8K 0.66–0.73 vs 0.70). 커밋 확신도는 수학에서 정답 예측(AUROC 0.749, CI가 0.5 제외)이나 사실 회상에선 null(0.471) — 보편이 아닌 영역별 신호. 모든 영역 풀링은 AUROC 0.437로 "확신할수록 더 자주 틀림"처럼 보이나 Simpson's-paradox 산물이지 발견이 아님. 샘플러는 공격적: 생성이 3.3–17.1 accept-call(48스텝 예산 대비)에 엔트로피 0.002–0.012 nats — 0.1 한계보다 1–2자릿수 아래 — 로 끝. 정확도는 짝 AR 형제 gemma-4-26B-A4B-it와 동등(예: GSM8K 0.66–0.73 vs 0.70).

"Pooling commitments across regimes into a single AUROC gives 0.437, numerically below 0.5, which might be misread as 'more confident → more often wrong.' This is a Simpson's-paradox artifact."

5DiscussionDiscussion

The title is the conclusion: neither parallel nor sequential — a partial, granularity-dependent left-to-right bias, regime-dependent (JSON ≈ order-independent), with an aggressive early-committing sampler and a confidence signal that's real only in some regimes, at AR-comparable accuracy. The paper is unusually honest about its own process: 제목이 곧 결론: 병렬도 순차도 아니다 — 부분적·해상도 의존 좌→우 편향, 영역 의존(JSON ≈ 순서 무관), 공격적 조기 커밋 샘플러, 일부 영역에서만 진짜인 확신 신호, AR과 동등한 정확도. 논문은 자기 과정에 유난히 정직하다:

"A strengthening run (5 seeds, prompt-clustered bootstrap, a block-size sweep, a block-sequential control, and a commit-non-monotonicity check) walked back our initial 'block-autoregressive' headline to this more careful claim."

— §8 (Conclusion) · arXiv:2606.14620 §8

13 threats to validity are listed (§6.1): τ_b is correlation, not mechanism (no causal claim); within-batch order is unresolved at accept-call resolution; commits aren't strictly frozen (4,524 un-accept events; robust on the monotone subset); block size is an analyst choice; small prompt counts / single checkpoint / single GPU; calibration is regime-specific; the AR comparison is accuracy-only and underpowered; R1–R3 may have pretraining contamination. Two measurement landmines are flagged: trailing-EOS padding (naive metric gives τ_b ≈ −0.9) and Simpson's-paradox pooling. 타당성 위협 13개(§6.1): τ_b는 상관이지 메커니즘 아님(인과 주장 없음); accept-call 해상도라 배치 내부 순서 미해결; 커밋이 엄격히 고정 아님(un-accept 4,524회; 단조 부분집합에선 견고); block size는 분석자 선택; 적은 프롬프트 수/단일 체크포인트/단일 GPU; 보정은 영역별; AR 비교는 정확도뿐이고 검정력 부족; R1–R3은 사전학습 오염 가능. 측정 지뢰 둘 표시: trailing-EOS 패딩(순진 지표는 τ_b ≈ −0.9), Simpson's-paradox 풀링.

Two takeaways we'd carry: (1) the aggressive early stop (finishing in ≪48 steps at near-zero entropy) is the real speed lever in practice — the step budget is rarely spent; (2) the methodology (EOS padding, ties, Simpson's paradox, block-size sweeps) is a reusable checklist for any "is this model parallel?" measurement, including our own benchmarking.
